Nile Delta Ground Deformation Dynamics

Egypt's Nile Delta contains critical infrastructure, agricultural land, coastal communities, and major industrial centers. Many of these areas rest on low-lying, sediment-rich terrain vulnerable to land movement driven by sediment compaction, groundwater extraction, and urban loading.

Analysis reveals that the Delta is not moving as a uniform block. The northern region exhibits subsidence ranging generally from 0 to -7 mm/year, with localized hotspots reaching up to -17 mm/year.

Field Inspection Hotspots

Ground-truthing and field investigations at high-subsidence locations documented visible structural damage including cracks, leaning structures, and foundation degradation.

Ashtoum Al-Gamil Bridge, Port Said

Severe structural fracturing and concrete damage observed along bridge support piers.

Muharram Bek Bus Station, Alexandria

Floor tiles displacement and deep horizontal wall cracking near foundation levels.

El Mataria, Ad Daqahliyah

Residential area impact near Lake Manzala due to low-lying wetland conditions.
